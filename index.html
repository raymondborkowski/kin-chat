Kin-Chat

Never miss a birthday or double-buy a gift. Your family's birthdays, wishlists and plans, in one place.

Getting started

  1. One person downloads Kin-Chat, signs in with Apple and taps Start our family.
  2. They add everyone, kids included, then tap Send the Invite to share the family code.
  3. Everyone else signs in, taps I have a code, enters it and picks their own name.

Common questions

Can the person whose wishlist it is see who's buying what?

No. You can't see claims on your own wishlist. Everyone else can, so nobody doubles up.

Do kids need their own account?

No. Kids are added by their parents, who keep their birthday, sizes and wishlist up to date. Only kids 13 and older with their own phone should sign in themselves.

How do I stop or change notifications?

In the app, go to Family › Notifications. You can turn each kind of reminder on or off.

How do I delete my account?

Family › Delete My Account. If nobody else in your family has signed in, this deletes the whole family. See the privacy policy for details.
