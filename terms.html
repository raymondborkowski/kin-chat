Terms of Use

Last updated November 14, 2026

Kin-Chat is a private space for your family. By signing in, you agree to these terms.

Be kind

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior in Kin-Chat. Don't post anything that is hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, violent, threatening, or illegal, and don't bully or impersonate anyone.

Reporting and blocking

  • To report a post, tap … on it and choose Report Post. To report a person, open their profile and tap Report.
  • To block someone, tap … on one of their posts or open their profile and tap Block. You won't see their posts or get alerts about them.
  • We review every report within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these terms and remove anyone who posted it from Kin-Chat.

Your content

You own what you post. You give Kin-Chat permission to store it and show it to the members of your family, which is all it's used for. You can delete your posts at any time, and deleting your account removes your content. See the privacy policy.

Kids

Parents add and manage their kids' profiles. Kids under 13 shouldn't sign in on their own.

Changes and contact

If these terms change, we'll update this page. Questions or concerns: send us a request.
