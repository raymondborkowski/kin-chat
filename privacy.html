Kin-Chat

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Kin-Chat is a private app for one family to keep birthdays, wishlists, plans, photos and stories in one place. This policy explains what Kin-Chat stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Only people who join your family with its family code can see your family's information.
  • There are no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. Your information is never sold or shared with advertisers.
  • You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

What Kin-Chat stores

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Apple. Kin-Chat receives an identifier for your account and, if you allow it, your name and email address (which may be an Apple private relay address).
  • Family details you or your family add. Names, households, addresses, birthdays, phone numbers, clothing sizes, allergies, notes, and profile photos. A family member can add these for someone else, for example a parent adding a child.
  • Things you share. Photos, captions, answers to the question of the day (including voice recordings and their transcripts), reactions, wishlist items, gift claims, recipes, gatherings, RSVPs and date votes.
  • Step counts, only if you choose. If you tap "Update my steps from Apple Health" in the family league, Kin-Chat reads your total step count for the current week and shares that one number with your family. It never writes to Apple Health and reads nothing else from it.
  • A notification token for your device, so Kin-Chat can send you alerts you've turned on.

Who can see it

Everything above is visible only to the members of your family who have joined with your family code, with two deliberate exceptions that keep gifts a surprise: you cannot see who has claimed items on your own wishlist, and in a gift exchange each person can see only who they are buying for.

Kin-Chat does not show your information to anyone outside your family.

Where it's kept

Family information is stored with Supabase, our database and file-storage provider, on servers in the United States. It is encrypted in transit. Supabase processes it only to provide the service to Kin-Chat. Notifications are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service.

If you turn on "Save family photos to Photos", Kin-Chat copies family photos into an album on your own device. Speech-to-text for voice answers uses Apple's speech recognition.

Children

Kin-Chat is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 should not sign in themselves. Parents and other family members can add a child's name, birthday, sizes and wishlist to the family directory so relatives know what to get them; that information is managed by the family, not by the child.

Deleting your information

  • Delete your account in the app under Family › Delete My Account. This removes your sign-in, your profile, your wishlist and your posts. If you are the last member of your family who has signed in, the entire family and everything in it is deleted too.
  • Any member can remove a family member who hasn't signed in, or delete their own posts, at any time.
  • Deleted information is removed from our database immediately. Backups kept by our provider expire on their normal schedule.
